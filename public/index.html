How to read a creator card

Each row shows a creator's full fact-check history at a glance. Click any row to see the individual videos. Two things matter most: whether the core argument held up, and the overall accuracy score.

Thesis Track Record

Each dot is one video. The real question isn't whether a creator makes some mistakes — it's whether their central argument survives scrutiny. Green (INTACT) means the core claim holds. Orange (COMPROMISED) means load-bearing errors were found. Red (COLLAPSED) means the thesis fails under the evidence.

Accuracy Score

The percentage of verifiable claims that checked out — weighted by importance. Core claims count 3×, supporting claims 2×, background context 1×. A creator can score well on facts but still have a COMPROMISED thesis if the key claims fail.

What gets checked

Every factual claim in the video is extracted and rated True, False / Misleading, or Disputed (genuine scholarly disagreement). We also flag rhetorical tricks and document the strongest counterargument the speaker didn't address. Sources are linked for every false claim.
