## SECTION B — Schopenhauer Stratagem Analysis

### Stratagem 30 — Appeal to Authority (Argumentum ad Verecundiam)

**Timestamp:** 12:30–12:56
**Quote:** **"The conscious mind believe it or not operates at a remarkably low bandwidth about 40 to 44 bits per second... the subconscious mind which is like a high velocity supercomputer or quantum computer that can process roughly 11 million bits of information per second."**

Abke cites specific neuroscience figures without attribution to lend empirical credibility to his metaphysical framework. The figures derive from Manfred Zimmermann's work on sensory nerve bandwidth (popularized by Tor Nørretranders in *The User Illusion*, 1998), but the 11 million figure describes total sensory nerve input — not "subconscious processing capacity." The scientific data is real but structurally repurposed to support a claim it was not designed to support: that literal personality "selves" live and operate in the subconscious.

### Stratagem 38 — Become Personal / Appeal to Identity

**Timestamp:** 2:53
**Quote:** **"And if you're spiritually awake, you might also be aware of the branches."**

This creates an in-group/out-group dynamic: those who agree with the framework are "spiritually awake"; those who do not are implicitly asleep. Questioning the model is subtly framed as a failure of awareness rather than a legitimate intellectual disagreement.

### Stratagem 11 — Induction with Concealed Counterexamples

**Timestamp:** 9:42–10:03
**Quote:** **"A self cannot do anything outside of this sequence and every self goes about their sequence in unique ways. But that's why it's important to see the core pattern of all selves. Lack, attachment, control. It is always these same three beliefs."**

The claim that every psychological identity operates through lack → attachment → control conceals obvious counterexamples. Adaptive identities — a professional self that develops mastery through genuine interest, a parental self motivated by love, a creative self driven by curiosity — do not arise from perceived deficiency. Abke's model is presented as universal without acknowledging cases where identity formation is healthy, growth-oriented, or non-defensive.

### Stratagem 36 — Bewildering with Empty Words (Argumentum ad Auditores)

**Timestamp:** 1:51–2:08
**Quote:** **"And aggregores work the exact same way in your own mind. Detrimental structures don't directly control your thoughts and behaviors, but they do so through the internal enforcers they generate in your mind called selves."**

"Egregores," "detrimental structures," "simatic pattern," and "4D University" are deployed as established concepts without rigorous definition. Egregore is an occult/esoteric term (from Éliphas Lévi, 19th-century French occultism) presented as though it names a real, recognized phenomenon. The listener receives a sense of depth without verifiable content.

### Stratagem 26 — Retorsio Argumenti (Turning the Tables)

This inversion is rhetorically striking but not argued for — it is asserted. The claim that "the ego has you" rather than "you have an ego" functions as a reframe, not a proof. No evidence is supplied for why this reversal is more accurate than the conventional framing.

## SECTION F — Most Likely False or Misleading Claims

### 1. Muscle testing can reprogram the subconscious mind
**Rating:** Likely false — applied kinesiology has no scientific support as a diagnostic or therapeutic tool. **Confidence: High.**
**Evidence:** A 2014 double-blind study found muscle testing performed at chance level (53%, P = .258) for identifying substances ([PubMed 24607076](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24607076/)). The American Cancer Society and the Australian Government's Department of Health both found no evidence of effectiveness.
**What would settle it:** A well-designed RCT demonstrating muscle testing's efficacy for belief identification or subconscious reprogramming.
**Timestamp + Quote:** 4:16 — **"I'm going to be doing a 4-hour workshop on this exact topic. How to truly change your beliefs on all four of these levels and reprogram your subconscious mind using tools like muscle testing."**

### 2. The subconscious mind processes roughly 11 million bits of information per second
**Rating:** Misleading — the 11 million figure describes total sensory nerve input bandwidth (Zimmermann, 1989), not subconscious "processing." **Confidence: High.**
**Evidence:** Manfred Zimmermann's original data, popularized in Nørretranders' *The User Illusion* (1998), measures bandwidth of sensory afferent nerves entering the brain — not the processing capacity of a "subconscious mind." The figure has been widely misappropriated in self-help literature. ([Quora discussion with citations](https://www.quora.com/Is-it-true-that-our-unconscious-mind-can-process-information-up-to-11-million-bits-per-second))
**What would settle it:** Peer-reviewed measurement of subconscious processing capacity independent of sensory input bandwidth.
**Timestamp + Quote:** 12:47 — **"The subconscious mind which is like a high velocity supercomputer or quantum computer that can process roughly 11 million bits of information per second."**

### 3. "Awareness and unconsciousness cannot coexist in the same space anymore than light and darkness"
**Rating:** Misleading — this absolutist claim contradicts well-established findings in cognitive psychology. **Confidence: High.**
**Evidence:** Unconscious processes (implicit biases, priming, automatic motor programs) continue running even during heightened conscious awareness. Mindfulness meditation increases awareness of some patterns while the subconscious continues autonomous operation. The metaphor of "light and darkness" suggests total dissolution, which does not occur.
**What would settle it:** Already settled by existing cognitive science — conscious and unconscious processes operate in parallel, not in mutual exclusion.
**Timestamp + Quote:** 23:08 — **"Awareness and unconsciousness cannot coexist in the same space anymore than light and darkness can coexist in the same space."**

## SECTION H — Mostly True & Fair Claims

1. **The ego is not a physical entity** — Neuroscience confirms there is no discrete "ego organ"; the sense of self is a distributed neurological process (Metzinger, *Being No One*, 2003).
2. **The conscious mind processes approximately 40–50 bits per second** — Derived from Zimmermann (1989) and popularized by Nørretranders (1998); the figure is widely accepted in information-theoretic neuroscience.
3. **Reading one line of text is a fair analogy for conscious bandwidth** — At ~300 words per minute (50 bits/sec), one line per second is a reasonable simplification.
4. **Beliefs influence behavior patterns** — A foundational principle of cognitive-behavioral therapy (Beck, 1976).
5. **Defense mechanisms, addiction, and avoidance are belief-driven behaviors** — Well-established across psychodynamic, cognitive, and behavioral frameworks.
6. **People experience different aspects of personality in different contexts** — Social psychology's "context-dependent self" is empirically documented (Markus & Wurf, 1987).
7. **The mind creates sub-personalities or "parts" in response to perceived threats** — Central to Internal Family Systems therapy (Schwartz, 1995) and consistent with structural dissociation theory.
8. **Perceived lack of safety can generate defensive/protective identity patterns** — Core concept in attachment theory (Bowlby, 1969) and IFS.
9. **Perceived lack of worth can generate overachieving/compensatory patterns** — Supported by Adlerian psychology (inferiority complex) and schema therapy (Young, 2003).
10. **Perceived lack of love can generate people-pleasing patterns** — Documented in codependency literature and attachment theory (anxious attachment style).
11. **Subconscious patterns influence physical health and behavior** — Established in psychosomatic medicine and health psychology.
12. **Conscious awareness of unconscious patterns reduces their automatic influence** — Supported by mindfulness-based cognitive therapy (Segal, Williams & Teasdale, 2002) and psychodynamic research on insight.
13. **Suppression of psychological patterns is counterproductive** — Wegner's "ironic process theory" (1994) demonstrates thought suppression paradoxically increases the suppressed thought's frequency.
14. **Self-compassion facilitates psychological healing** — Extensively documented by Kristin Neff's self-compassion research and Compassion-Focused Therapy (Gilbert, 2009).
15. **The book *The Three Beliefs of Ego* exists** — Confirmed; published by New World Library, available in paperback, Kindle, and audiobook.
